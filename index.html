⚖️ Universal Principles

The 5 Laws of Machine Learning

The governing axioms woven throughout the entire book that bridge theory to production mastery.

Law 1

Prediction ≠ Understanding

A high accuracy model fits historical patterns, but mapping is not causal truth. Beware of spurious correlation.

Law 2

All Models Are Wrong, Some Generalize

The Bias-Variance tradeoff is unavoidable. Optimization without regularization is just memorizing noise.

Law 3

Computation & Data Beat Cleverness

Rich representation and volume consistently outperform complex hand-crafted heuristic rules.

Law 4

The Metric You Optimize Is A Proxy

Goodhart's Law: when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Monitor business outcomes.

Law 5

Deployment Is Just The Beginning

Data drifts, environments shift, and feedback loops evolve. Constant monitoring and retraining are vital.
